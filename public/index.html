How It Works

Step 1: Enter Your Contact Number

Provide your contact number to receive a One-Time Password (OTP) that ensures the security of your verification process.

Step 2: Verify Your OTP

After receiving the OTP, enter it into the system to confirm your identity and gain access to the verification platform.

Step 3: Upload Your Documents

Upload your documents, such as your citizenship certificate, for real-time verification against the government database.

Step 4: Document Verification

Our system automatically checks your documents against a secure backend database to verify their authenticity.

Step 5: Get Your Results

Once verified, you'll receive the status of your document's authenticity, ensuring you can trust the results for all your needs.

Step 6: Download Your Verified Documents

If your documents are verified successfully, you can download the verified copy for your records or to share with other parties.
