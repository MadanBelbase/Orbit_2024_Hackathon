Frequently Asked Questions

What is this platform?

This platform allows you to securely verify your documents using OTP verification.

How do I use the app?

Simply enter your contact number, verify OTP, and upload your documents for verification.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes, we prioritize your privacy and use strong encryption to protect your personal information.

How long does the verification process take?

The verification process usually takes just a few minutes, depending on document type and volume.

Can I use this platform for business purposes?

Yes, businesses such as hotels and other establishments can use this platform to verify citizenship numbers and prevent fraud.

Do I need to pay for the verification service?

Basic verification is free of charge. However, there may be additional fees for advanced features or bulk verifications.

What types of documents can be verified?

You can verify a variety of government-issued documents, such as citizenship certificates and ID cards.

Can I track the status of my verification?

Yes, you will receive notifications and updates about the status of your document verification.

Is there a mobile app available?

Currently, our platform is web-based, but we are planning to release a mobile app in the future.

What should I do if I encounter an issue with the platform?

If you encounter any issues, please contact our support team through the 'Contact Us' section, and we'll assist you promptly.
