Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of this system?

This system is designed to verify the authenticity of documents submitted to government authorities and identify fraudulent activities.

Who can use this verification system?

The system is intended for use by government departments, authorized personnel, and individuals submitting documents for verification.

What types of documents can be verified?

The system supports verification of various document types, including identity cards, certificates, licenses, and more.

How does the verification process work?

The submitted document is scanned and cross-checked against a secured database using advanced fraud detection algorithms.

Is my data secure?

Yes, the system uses end-to-end encryption and complies with government data protection regulations to ensure the security and privacy of your information.
